- 🌙 **GitHub Nightlight Theme** - Deep slate backgrounds with neon blue accents
- 🔮 **Glassmorphism Design** - Modern semi-transparent panels and cards

- 💎 **Premium Feel** - Every component redesigned for a modern experience
